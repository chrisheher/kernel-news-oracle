The old books saw it coming

What can a library of old crusty books say about what’s going on today? I used kernel to pull current events, then match them with passages in my GraphRAG library that called their shot.

  1. 01 · Readkernel browsers crawl 8 news sitesOne stealth cloud browser, driven by Playwright, reads the recent stories.
  2. 02 · MatchEmbeddings search the libraryEach story is compared with ~1,600 passages from ~230 literary works in my library.
  3. 03 · PickTwo books per storyNo book is cited twice, and duplicate coverage of one story is dropped.
  4. 04 · ExplainClaude writes each connectionOne plain sentence on what the old passage anticipates or reframes.
